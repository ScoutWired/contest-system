Help & participation rules

  • Team members sign in with Discord; guests use issued usernames + access codes.
  • New proposals stay private until moderated and published.
  • One active vote per person per poll. Vote changes only when the poll allows it.
  • Abstentions are reported separately and never decide winners. Ties are reported as TIED.
  • Votes by later-revoked accounts remain counted (audit-logged); revocation blocks future votes.
